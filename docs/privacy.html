Privacy Policy

Focus Gate

Last updated September 21, 2026

Short version: Focus Gate collects nothing, sends nothing anywhere, and has no server. Everything it stores stays on your own device, in your own browser.

What the extension does

Focus Gate is a personal productivity tool. Before a page loads on a site you've configured (by default: Instagram, Reddit, YouTube), it asks "is this for work?". If you say no, the page doesn't load. If you say yes, it's unlocked for a grace period you set, then asks again.

What data it stores

  • Your configured site list, prompt text and grace period — stored via chrome.storage.local.
  • Per-tab "currently unlocked" state — stored via chrome.storage.session, cleared automatically when the browser closes.
  • Usage insights: minutes spent on gated pages per day, and counts of how many times you answered yes/no — stored via chrome.storage.local so the built-in Insights page can show you a summary.

All of the above lives only in your local Chrome profile. It is never transmitted anywhere — there is no backend server, no analytics SDK, and no network request made by this extension at all.

What data it does not collect

  • No browsing history beyond the gated sites you configure.
  • No page content, form data, keystrokes, or personal information.
  • No account, sign-in, or identifying information of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any third party — there is no third party involved.

Permissions

  • tabs / webNavigation — to detect when you navigate to a configured site and redirect the tab to the confirmation prompt before the page loads.
  • storage — to save your settings and usage stats locally.
  • alarms — to re-lock a page after your grace period elapses.
  • idle — to avoid logging usage time while your computer is idle.

Uninstalling

Removing the extension (chrome://extensions → Remove) deletes all of its stored data along with it.
